Operating model

How money moves from treasury to science

One vehicle, two pools with different jobs, and a venture builder that does the hands-on work.

Step 1

Capital in

Qualified investors commit once to a regulated Hong Kong fund vehicle.

Step 2

Treasury engine

Bitcoin plus a T-bill reserve of 42.9% at launch, with no Bitcoin sold in a down quarter.

Step 3

Spending rule

The science budget follows smoothed treasury value, starting near US$1.5M a year at the 3% floor.

Step 4

Milestone gate

An independent scientific board verifies each milestone before the next tranche is released.

Value flows back

Licence upfronts, milestone income and capped revenue shares from companies that already sell.

Investors share the cycle

A managed distribution moves with the treasury, so losses are not passed to the science.

Venture builder

Jurisdiction · Hong Kong

Why we are building in Hong Kong

Hong Kong combines a simple tax system, ready-made fund structures, a licensed regime for digital assets and Asia’s deepest market for biotech listings.

Summary only; the final structure is subject to Hong Kong tax and legal advice.

Low, simple tax

Profits tax of 8.25% on the first HK$2 million and 16.5% above it. No capital gains tax and no withholding tax on dividends.

Fund structures

Open-ended fund companies and limited partnership funds, with a unified fund tax exemption. A 2026 bill would add digital assets; it is not yet law.

Digital-asset rules

The Securities and Futures Commission licenses managers and platforms, a regulated route to Bitcoin exposure.

Biotech capital

84 biotech companies listed under HKEX Chapter 18A by January 2026, raising more than US$17.5 billion since 2018.

Gateway to Asia

Close to Mainland China and Asian licensees, and home of the AXION Technology Foundation, co-founded by Agile Dynamics.

Governance

The people who pick deals do not hold or value the money

A board with an independent majority, three committees and independent providers keep governance, investment and operations apart.

Board of Directors

Independent majority; owns strategy and risk.

Investment Committee

Approves every tranche. The sponsor cannot approve its own deals.

Scientific Advisory Board

Verifies each milestone and has no vote on capital.

Custodian and manager

An independent custodian holds the assets; an SFC-licensed manager runs the mandate.

Roadmap

Four phases from formation to first deals
